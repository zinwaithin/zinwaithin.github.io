Description


Early life and education

Cristiano Ronaldo dos Santos Aveiro was born on 5 February 1985 in Funchal, Madeira, Portugal. He grew up in a working-class neighborhood and started playing football at a very young age. Recognizing his incredible talent, he joined the youth academy of Sporting CP when he was just 12 years old. He dedicated his entire youth to football training and professional development, making his senior debut for Sporting CP in 2002.


Career

Ronaldo's professional career took off when he transferred to Manchester United in 2003 under Sir Alex Ferguson. He won his first Ballon d'Or and Champions League title there. In 2009, he made a historic transfer to Real Madrid for a world-record fee. At Real Madrid, he became the club's all-time top scorer, winning 4 Champions League titles and establishing himself as one of the greatest football players in history.

In 2018, he moved to Juventus, winning consecutive Serie A titles, before briefly returning to Manchester United in 2021. Internationally, Ronaldo led Portugal to victory in UEFA Euro 2016 and the 2019 Nations League. In 2023, he joined the Saudi Pro League club Al Nassr, where he continues to break goalscoring records and inspire millions of fans around the world.
