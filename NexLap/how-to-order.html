Order process

From choosing to confirmation.

Follow these five steps when ordering from NexLap.

01

Choose

Choose a laptop

Browse laptops by brand or how you plan to use them, then choose a suitable option for your needs and budget.

Browse laptops Shop by use
02

Submit

Send your order details

Provide your name, phone number, selected laptop and preferred delivery or store pickup method.

03

Confirm

NexLap confirms the order

NexLap checks the selected model, current availability and final price before asking you to complete payment.

Do not make payment before receiving confirmation.
04

Pay

Complete MMQR payment

After the order is confirmed, complete payment using the provided MMQR and keep a clear screenshot of your payment receipt.

05

Receive

Receive order confirmation

NexLap verifies the payment and confirms your order, delivery or pickup details.
