ABOUT LAVRO

Making remarkable spaces easier to experience.

LAVRO is a Laos-based spatial media company specializing in 360° virtual experiences. We help businesses and institutions present their spaces with clarity, context, and confidence.

Our approach is simple: capture accurately, present beautifully, and make it easy for people to understand a space — anytime, anywhere.
